# II. Appendix A – Defense 1.1 Targets (Proposed at Review 2) & BM04 Commitment Baseline

## 9.1 Main Workflows (4 Workflows = 1 per Team Member)

Every Main Workflow spans `>= 2` human/system actors, exercises `>= 3` mandatory features, drives end-to-end state transitions across main domain entities, and traces 1-to-1 to an Activity Diagram in **Report 4 SDD (§3.0.1–§3.0.4)**:

| ID | Workflow Name & SDD Trace | Actors Involved | Business Outcome | Mandatory Features | Entity State Transitions | Owner |
| :---: | --- | --- | --- | :---: | --- | :---: |
| **`WF-01`** | **Enterprise Onboarding, Drone Equipment Leasing & Recurring Schedule Dispatch** *(SDD §3.4.3)* | `Organization Manager`, `Platform Manager`, `System` | Customer organization is onboarded with an active Enterprise subscription, leased drone provisioned into fleet, infrastructure asset registered with checkpoints, and recurring schedule generating an assigned inspection. | `FE-02`, `FE-03`, `FE-04` | `Subscription`: `Pending -> Active`<br>`DroneLease`: `Pending -> Active`<br>`PlatformDrone`: `Available -> Leased`<br>`Drone`: `-> Active (Leased)`<br>`Asset`: `-> Active`<br>`RecurringInspectionSchedule`: `-> Active`<br>`ScheduleOccurrence`: `-> Generated`<br>`Inspection`: `-> Assigned` | **Trần Hoàng Trung Hiếu** (`SE184212`) |
| **`WF-02`** | **Mission Dispatch, Self-Service Flight Permit Gate & On-Site Field Survey Execution** *(SDD §3.6.3)* | `Organization Manager`, `Inspector` (Web & Mobile), `System` | Inspection mission is dispatched without resource conflict, flight readiness is cleared via uploaded flight permit and valid pilot license, on-site field session is completed against asset checkpoints, and EXIF-geotagged aerial evidence is stored in MinIO. | `FE-05`, `FE-06`, `FE-07` | `Inspection`: `Assigned -> Ready for Flight -> In Progress -> Field Completed`<br>`FieldSession`: `Planned -> In Progress -> Postponed -> In Progress -> Field Completed`<br>`Evidence`: `-> Active (is_deleted=false)` | **Phùng Trung Quốc** (`SE170027`) |
| **`WF-03`** | **Advisory YOLO11 Defect Verification, Report Publication & Corrective Handoff** *(SDD §3.9.3)* | `Inspector`, `YOLO11 / LLM`, `Organization Manager` (Independent Reviewer) | Field evidence is analyzed by YOLO11 (`MBDD2025`), findings are verified and snapshot-locked by the Inspector, report sections are drafted and verified, an independent Manager approves and publishes the immutable PDF report, and repair-required findings are forwarded to maintenance. | `FE-01`, `FE-08`, `FE-09` | `Finding`: `Pending -> Confirmed`<br>`Inspection`: `Field Completed -> Report Draft -> Report Published -> Repair Pending`<br>`InspectionReport`: `Draft -> Author Verified -> Submitted -> Approved -> Published` | **Trần Tùng Bách** (`SE180220`) |

| **`DS-01b`** | `WF-01` | **Exception / Guard** | `Platform Admin` session; `DJI-M350-POOL-01` already in `Leased` status; `Bridge Pier B-01` already has an `Active` recurring schedule. | 1. `Platform Admin` attempts to confirm subscription/lease payment.<br>2.
