## 1. Summary of Scope Evolution Rationale
Following committee feedback and full-stack implementation across `SmartDroneInspection-backend`, `-frontend`, and `-mobile`, the project scope was refined into the **5-Role Platform Inspection Service model** and narrowed to **8 balanced product features (`FE-01` to `FE-08`, 2 features per team member)** while preserving the approved project code (`GFA26SE139`) and title:
1. **Transition to the Platform-Managed Inspection Service Model (`MF1`–`MF4`)**: Replaced external `SmartDroneHub` dependencies and self-service SaaS drone leasing with a realistic B2B **Platform Inspection Service** model where Customer organizations register airspace-verified infrastructure assets, submit one-time or recurring Service Requests, negotiate VND/USD Quotations via Order Chat, sign paper contracts (`CONTRACT_SIGNED`), and pay online via **PayOS (VietQR)** after the Platform Manager publishes the verified PDF report, while the Platform coordinates vetted third-party Partner Inspectors and their 3-document registered owned drones.
2. **Five Canonical Roles Across Three Operational Zones**: Standardized the 5 login roles into `ADMIN` (`Platform Admin`), `MANAGER` (`Platform Manager`), `CUSTOMER` (`Customer Organization Owner/Manager`), `INSPECTOR` (`Partner Drone Inspector`), and `MAINTENANCE_ENGINEER` (`Customer Maintenance Engineer`) across `PLATFORM`, `INSPECTOR_PARTNER`, and `CUSTOMER_ORGANIZATION` zones (`V7`).
3. **Narrowed 8-Feature Structure (`FE-01` to `FE-08`, 2 Features per Member)**: Consolidated the product WBS from 12 granular features into **8 high-cohesion features (`FE-01`–`FE-08`, 16 functions total)** mapped 1-to-1 to the 8 test sheets (`Feature 1`–`Feature 8`) in `Report5_Test Report.xlsx`, with each of the 4 team members owning exactly 2 features and 1 Main Workflow (`WF-01`–`WF-04`).
4. **Streamlined Field, Reporting & RAG Maintenance Workflows (`V10`–`V17`)**: Streamlined `InspectionStatus` to 6 states (`SCHEDULED`, `IN_PROGRESS`, `FIELD_COMPLETED`, `UNDER_REVIEW`, `COMPLETED`, `CANCELLED`), replaced session postpone with `Abort` + Manager re-dispatch (`Reschedule`, `Reassign`, `Cancel`), added streaming `video/mp4` evidence intake, streamlined `ReportStatus` to 5 auto-compiled states (`SUBMITTED`, `RETURNED`, `APPROVED`, `PUBLISHED`, `SUPERSEDED`) with an AI Executive Summary card, and enhanced `MF4` with 1-click RAG initial tasks, RAG cost norms (`suggest-estimate`), 1-click batch work-log verification (`batch-verify`), smart auto-drafted completion reports (`auto-draft`), RAG technical acceptance compliance audit (`acceptance-compliance`), and the 3-step/3-way Re-inspection defect verification loop (`reinspection_defect_verifications`).
